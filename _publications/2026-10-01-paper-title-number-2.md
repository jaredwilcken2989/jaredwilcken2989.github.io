## Conference Paper

**Radio Implementation and Interception of a McEliece-Based Cryptosystem**

### Authors

### Research Overview

This paper explores the implementation and interception of a McEliece-based cryptosystem using radio communication, examining the practical application of post-quantum cryptography in wireless systems.
